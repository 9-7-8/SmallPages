word puzzles

Give it a list of words — with clues, if you have them — and it builds the puzzle around them: a crossword, a word search, a criss-cross, a scramble, fill-the-blanks, a matching sheet, a code cracker or bingo cards. The grid is worked out in the page, and the sheet prints as it looks, answer key and all.
