remove background

Drop in a photo and the subject comes out on transparency, cut by a segmentation model that runs on your own machine. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Starting

The cut is made by @imgly/background-removal, an ISNet segmentation model running under ONNX Runtime, bundled into this repo so the page doesn't depend on anyone else's script host. This page asks it for the full-precision model rather than the quantized default: it's the sharpest of the three, and about 80 MB to fetch the first time. Your browser caches it after that, and the picture itself never leaves the page.
