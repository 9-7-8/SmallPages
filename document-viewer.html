Document Viewer

Drop in a document and read it here — logs, plain text and Markdown, config and data files like JSON, XML, TOML, CSV and TSV, with PDF, Word and saved email too. An .eml opens as the message: its headers, its text or its HTML with the pictures that came inside it, and its attachments to save or open here — pictures hosted elsewhere stay unfetched until you ask, since that is what tells the sender you read it. Find walks the matches one at a time; the line filters keep only the lines that match a string or a regular expression, or drop the ones that do, with as many lines either side as you want. A big file is read a chunk at a time and drawn a screen at a time, so size isn't the thing that stops it.

Drop a document here, or click to choose one
.log .txt .md .json .xml .toml .csv .tsv .config .settings — any text at all — plus .pdf, .docx and .eml · opened in your browser, never uploaded

Find reads the lines themselves rather than the handful on screen, which is the only way to reach past what's drawn — Ctrl+F puts the cursor in it, Enter walks forward, Shift+Enter back, Esc clears. It searches what the filters have left on screen. The filters themselves are on /.

A line is shown when it matches at least one Include rule — or when there are none — and no Exclude rule. Turn on .* to read the pattern as a regular expression. Before and After keep that many neighbouring lines either side of every line a rule kept, the way grep -B and -A do; they come through dimmed, and they come whether or not they would have been kept on their own. Copy and download take whatever is on screen, context and all.
