Sheet → Pieces

Drop in a sheet of many things — a sticker sheet, a game UI kit, a page of frames or icons — and every separate thing on it comes out as its own picture on transparency, cut whole. The backdrop is flooded away from the edges the way Remove Background does it, then whatever is left is gathered into pieces: shapes close together stay one piece, so a frame keeps its corner flourishes and an icon keeps its label. Save them one at a time or all at once as a zip. It all runs on your own machine, and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

The backdrop is the colour most of the sheet's border agrees on, or a fake-transparency checkerboard of two greys. A thin frame line or bar round the very edge of a sheet would hide it, so the border is read again a few pixels in when the outermost ring doesn't agree. A sheet whose backdrop isn't one colour (a screenshot, a painted page) can't be split this way — Remove Background's models are the tool for that.

Joining works on the clearly-coloured pixels only: faint marks just past the backdrop — a stock site's watermark, the grey halo JPEG leaves round shapes — would otherwise bridge one thing to the next and glue half the sheet together. Raise Ignore faint marks if pieces still come out stuck together, lower it if a pale piece goes missing. Each piece still keeps all of its own soft edge. Things whose artwork really touches stay one piece. A piece marked edge runs off the sheet itself, so it was cut by the picture, not here.
