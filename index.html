SmallPages

Single-purpose tools that run entirely in the browser. Nothing uploads anywhere.

Pictures

Something in, a picture out.

  • Anything → Image Paste or drop anything — PDFs and spreadsheets too — get a picture
  • Image Compressor Shrink a picture and wipe between before and after
  • Image Grid A pile of pictures laid out as one sheet, so many across
  • Image → SVG Trace a bitmap to vector shapes or lines
  • Image Upscaler Enlarge a picture with a model, and drag the seam to see what it added
  • Make Cassette Image A picture in the window of a VHS tape, titled on the label
  • Make CD Image Put a picture on a disc, under real gloss and tape
  • PNG → Swirl GIF Spin a still image into a loop
  • Quote Maker A quote over a photograph, at your screen's resolution or a post's
  • Remove Background Cut the subject out of a photo
  • Sheet → Pieces Cut every separate thing on a sticker sheet or UI kit out whole
  • Uncrop Put a photo on a bigger canvas and have the new border painted in
  • Video → Animation A silent loop as a GIF, WebP, APNG or WebM

Colour

Picking colours, and making them agree.

  • Colour Picker Pick, sample, match to thread and Pantone
  • Palette → Gem Grow a faceted stone from a palette
  • Unified Palette Cross-mix colours into a set that agrees

Paper

Documents, and the pages inside them.

  • Document Viewer Read a log, PDF, Word file or saved email, and filter it by line
  • Images → Searchable PDF A pile of scans into one document you can search
  • PDF Builder Merge, reorder and drop pages across documents
  • PDF OCR Read the words off a scanned document and put them back into it
  • Tile PDF Pages Several pages side by side on one big sheet

Words

Text to write, read back, or measure.

  • Blank Page Somewhere to write, with nothing in the way
  • Captions → Transcript Subtitles into readable prose, stutter removed
  • Markdown → Rich Text Live preview, then copy it out formatted or save it
  • Quiz Converter An Anki deck or a quiz site’s CSV, turned into any of the others
  • Readability Check Grade level by five formulas, with the hard parts marked
  • Speed Reader A link or your own text, one word at a time, held still

Sound

Made in the tab, or turned into writing.

  • Audio → Text Transcribe a recording, with timestamps and subtitles
  • Mic Monitor Microphone straight back out, with a level meter
  • Noise Machine White, pink, brown, a fan, breath and beats, generated live
  • Playlist → Audio Build the yt-dlp command for a playlist
  • Rain Machine A storm on a map, heard from four places

At the table

Dice, hit points, and things to hand round.

  • Bingo Cards Your list, a card each, free squares from the middle out
  • Card Drawer A deck dealt onto the table — cards, tarot, Uno or your own
  • Combat Health Tracker Hit points big enough to read across a table, with the log underneath
  • Dice Roller XdY in, the dice thrown in 3D, and the odds behind them
  • Word Puzzles A crossword, a word search and six more from your own list

Games

Small ones, played in the tab.

  • Approach Route the aircraft down to their own strips without letting two touch
  • Festoon Swing the hanging lights until one colour takes the lot
  • Gridlock One-way roads between blobs that make cars and blobs that eat them

Minecraft

Textures, the way the game wants them.

  • GIF → .mcmeta Animated texture strip for Minecraft
  • Minecraft Item Pad to square, then every texture size

The day

Clocks, timers, and when to go to bed.

  • Countdown Minutes to go, or a date to reach, in figures that fill the page
  • Hex Clock The time, painted as the colour its own digits spell
  • Pomodoro Timer The whole page turns the colour of the phase
  • Sleep Calculator Bedtimes and wake-ups that land between cycles
  • Stopwatch Laps and splits to the hundredth, big enough to read across a room

Odds and ends

The ones that are only themselves.

  • Bookmarklets Little tools to drag to your bookmarks bar, source and all
  • Dragon Tree Saved Flight Rising pages into a family tree
  • House Style The theme itself, with both knobs live
  • Ruler A true-size rule and protractor, once you tell it how big the screen is
  • Unzip Open a zip and pull files out of it
