SmallPages

Single-purpose tools that run entirely in the browser. Nothing uploads anywhere.

  • GIF → .mcmeta Animated texture strip for Minecraft
  • Minecraft Item Pad to square, then every texture size
  • PNG → Swirl GIF Spin a still image into a loop
  • Image → SVG Trace a bitmap to vector shapes or lines
  • Remove Background Cut the subject out of a photo
  • Palette → Gem Grow a faceted stone from a palette
  • Unified Palette Cross-mix colours into a set that agrees
  • Playlist → Audio Build the yt-dlp command for a playlist
  • Audio → Text Transcribe a recording, with timestamps and subtitles
  • Mic Monitor Microphone straight back out, with a level meter
  • Anything → Image Paste or drop anything — a spreadsheet too — get a picture of it
  • Document Viewer Open a log, text or Word file and filter it by line
  • Colour Picker Pick, sample, match to thread and Pantone
  • Bingo Cards Your list, a card each, free squares from the middle out
  • Word Puzzles A crossword, a word search and six more from your own list
  • Readability Check Grade level by five formulas, with the hard parts marked
  • Speed Reader A link or your own text, one word at a time, held still
  • Noise Machine White, pink, brown, rain and a fan, generated live
  • Sleep Calculator Bedtimes and wake-ups that land between cycles
  • Image Compressor Shrink a picture and wipe between before and after
  • Hex Clock The time, painted as the colour its own digits spell
  • House Style The theme itself, with both knobs live
  • Unzip Open a zip and pull files out of it
