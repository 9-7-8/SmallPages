Skills & levelling

You have a character level on this server, separate from vanilla experience. It buys you stat points and the odd parcel of loot. It does not decide what you are allowed to touch.

Nothing is locked behind a level here. The RPG mod can gate items and blocks behind levels, and both of those lists on this server are empty — a stone pickaxe mines diamond on day one and a diamond sword works the moment you craft it. That was a deliberate change on 25 September after the gates were found to be doing nothing but annoying people. Carrot, not stick.

How levelling works

  • Where XP comes fromKilling things. It is the RPG mod's own pool, not your vanilla experience bar, so spending levels on enchanting costs you nothing here
  • The costLevel 1 is 140 points and each level after is a little dearer. The curve is steepest in the first twenty-five levels and flattens off after a hundred
  • The ceilingLevel 500, which nobody is going to reach. Treat the first fifty as the real game
  • DeathCosts you nothing. Levels and points are not reset

What a point buys

One point per level, spent across eight attributes. Each point is small on purpose — this is a slow curve rather than a power spike.

AttributePer point
Health+1 health, so half a heart
Attack damage+0.2, so +1 damage every five points
Attack speed+0.02
Armour+0.25, so +1 armour every four points
Movement speed+0.0025
Knockback resistance+1%
Armour toughness+0.1
Luck+0.1, so +1 luck every ten points

Every level also hands you something: coal and iron tools in the first few, diamonds and a golden apple by the teens, a full diamond set in the twenties and a totem at level 25. You do not have to go looking for it — it arrives when you level.

Spent your points badly? A respec gives all of them back and costs nothing, but it needs an operator to run it. Ask in chat rather than living with it.

The spell trees

Separately from your attributes, Pufferfish's Skills provides a skill-tree system and Skill Tree (RPG Series) fills it with the trees for the Wizards spells — a couple of hundred spells across arcane, fire and frost. If you want to cast rather than swing, that is the route in; the mods themselves are on the Magic page.
