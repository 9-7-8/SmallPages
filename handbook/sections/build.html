Building & decorating

This is the part of the pack that a browse of the recipe list undersells most. There are more than twenty decoration mods installed, and between them the palette is several times the vanilla one.

Shapes vanilla never gave you

  • EverySlabOver a thousand slabs, stairs, walls, fences and gates for the three hundred blocks that never had them. Start here — most “I can't make that shape” problems end on this mod
  • FramedBlocksFrames in every shape there is, which then take on the appearance of whatever block you camouflage them with. One mod, infinite textures
  • ChiselOne tool, and dozens of variants of each stone and metal. The cheapest way to stop everything looking like the same four blocks
  • Diagonal FencesFences that connect at 45°
  • Reconnectible ChainsRun a decorative chain between two fences or posts
  • Horizontal Reinforced DeepslateThe one block vanilla forgot to let you rotate

Furnishing it

  • Macaw'sTen separate mods — furniture, doors, windows, roofs, fences, paths, bridges, stairs, trapdoors and lights. The backbone of most builds here
  • MrCrayfish's Refurbished FurnitureHundreds of furniture pieces, and an electricity system so the appliances actually work
  • Ketket's FurnicraftA second, chunkier furniture set with a different feel
  • Faye's Rugs & Curtains and Pet FurnitureSoft furnishings, and beds and bowls for the animals
  • Arts and CraftsA broad decoration set — a good one to browse rather than describe
  • TerracottaThingsFunctional blocks made out of terracotta, for when the colour matters
  • PlushablesPlush toys. There is no mechanical reason for these and everyone makes them anyway

Light, sound and flags

Luminax and Moog's Glow Up add light sources that are not torches; Macaw's Lights covers lamps and lanterns. Windchimes and Tiny Flowers are small touches that make a place feel lived in. Banner Flags hangs your banners as proper flags, and Banner Tweaks makes designing one less painful — with Banner Capes you can wear it.

Building faster

  • Building Gadgets 2Place, copy, paste and exchange whole areas. The single biggest time saver in the pack for anything larger than a house
  • Construction SticksExtend a surface by pointing at it. Lighter-weight than the gadgets and often enough
  • MeasurementsMeasure a distance before you commit to it
  • Elevator Mod & Moving ElevatorsTwo kinds of vertical travel: an instant teleport pad, and a platform that actually moves. Both beat a ladder
  • Laser BridgesWalkable light bridges, for the gaps you don't want to fill
  • Double Doors & OpenableBoth halves of a double door open together, and more blocks open than used to

Paths are not just decoration. Every one of Macaw's 316 path blocks makes you about 40% faster to walk on, horses and carts included — the full rules are on Getting around. And you can step up a full block anywhere, on any block, without jumping.
