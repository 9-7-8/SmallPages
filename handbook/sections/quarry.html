Quarries

If you remember the old BuildCraft quarry, this pack has its direct descendant: QuarryPlus (the mod file is called AdditionalEnchantedMiner). Mark out a box, point a machine at it, give it power, and it strip-mines the whole thing down to bedrock while you do something else.

Start cheap — the quarry itself is endgame here

This pack rewrites the quarry recipe. Out of the box QuarryPlus is a mid-game machine, but a script in this pack replaces that recipe with an endgame one: allthemodium ingots, an unobtainium pickaxe, an eclipse alloy pickaxe, two productivity 4 upgrades and a Block Breaker T2. The Chunk Destroyer recipe is removed outright, and the Mini Quarry, Solid Fuel Quarry and Mining Well have no recipe in this pack at all. Do not plan around any of those.

So treat the quarry as something you build after allthemodium, not before. Until then there are two much cheaper ways to mine automatically:

  • Mining Gadget — 3 diamonds, 2 iron, 2 gold, redstone and an empty upgrade. A handheld laser that mines a configurable area, and takes Fortune, Silk Touch, efficiency and battery upgrades at a Modification Table. Cheapest real answer, and no chunk loading needed because you are standing there.
  • Block Breaker T1 (Just Dire Things) — 4 ferricore ingots, a diamond, 2 lapis, an observer and redstone. This is the automated one. T2 is an ingredient of the quarry itself, so building up through these is on the path anyway rather than wasted work.

Check the recipe in game before you gather for it. Press U on the machine in JEI. This pack overrides recipes from several mods, so a wiki or a video will often show you a recipe that is not the one here — which is exactly how the quarry catches people out.

Setting one up, start to finish

  1. Mark the area. Place a Marker Plus and right-click it to extend its beams, then place more until the beams enclose the box you want dug. A Flexible Marker does the same job from a single block — right-click it and type the size in, which is far less fiddly for a big area.
  2. Place the quarry against the marked area. It reads the box from the markers the moment you place it.
  3. Give it power. Run energy in from whatever you are using — it accepts ordinary FE from the pack's generators and cables.
  4. Let it build its frame. It lays a wireframe round the box first, then starts digging. This is normal and costs energy; nothing is wrong.
  5. Take the output away. A chest touching the quarry on any face is enough — the quarry pushes its drops into any adjacent inventory itself, in all six directions, so it does not have to be in front and you do not need pipes for a simple setup. Pipe or conduit it onward only if you want the items somewhere else. With nothing adjacent the drops pile up inside the quarry and it stalls when full — right-click the machine to see what it is holding.

A quarry only runs in a loaded chunk. Walk away and it stops. Claim the area and force-load it (see Claiming Land) or you will come back to a job that never progressed.

Modules worth adding

  • Quarry Pump Module — clears water and lava out of the way instead of grinding to a halt in an ocean.
  • Quarry Exp Collect Module — banks the XP the digging produces.
  • Void Module — throws away the cobble and dirt you do not want, so your storage fills with ore instead.
  • Filler Module — fills the hole back in behind itself.

Enchanting a quarry

This is the part people miss, and it is the single biggest upgrade available: QuarryPlus machines accept enchantments, exactly like a pickaxe.

  1. Enchant a pickaxe normally — or an enchanted book, if you are using the Book Enchantment Mover.
  2. Place an Enchantment Mover, put the enchanted pickaxe in one side and the quarry machine block in the other.
  3. Pick the enchantment and move it across. The machine keeps it permanently.

How far you can push it here

This server runs Apotheosis, which raises the vanilla caps. These are the actual limits on this server, read from the server itself:

  • Fortune 8 — not 10, and well above the vanilla 3.
  • Efficiency 9
  • Unbreaking 8
  • Looting 8
  • Silk Touch 1 — unchanged, and there is no higher version.

You cannot find Fortune 8 — you have to build it. Enchanting tables and loot only ever hand out up to Fortune 3 on this server. Everything above that comes from combining books and tools in an anvil, which is what Apotheosis' enchanting setup is for.

A quarry with Fortune multiplies every ore it touches, across an entire chunk, for as long as it runs. It is worth far more on a machine than on a pickaxe you swing by hand.

Fortune and Silk Touch do not stack. Pick one per machine. If you want both, run two quarries. QuarryPlus also has a FortuneBlockList and a SilktouchBlockList in its interface, so you can say which blocks get which treatment rather than applying it to everything.

If you would rather not build one

Mining Gadgets is in the pack too — a handheld laser that mines a configurable area, powered and upgradeable. Much less setup, much smaller scale, and it does not need chunk loading because you are standing there.
