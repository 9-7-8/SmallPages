Music discs

Turn any track on the internet into a playable disc, collect discs into albums, and play them somewhere everyone can hear. All of it comes from Music Disc Maker.

What you craft

  • Music Disc MakerA note block ringed by iron and lapis. This is the machine that writes a disc
  • Blank DiscAn iron ingot with lapis above, below and either side. You can also turn any ordinary music disc back into a blank one — just put it in the grid on its own
  • AlbumTwo leather and two paper, corner to corner. Holds a stack of discs in one item
  • Golden JukeboxEight gold ingots around a jukebox. The one with real controls — volume, range, repeat, shuffle, next and previous
  • BoomboxA jukebox with iron, wool and redstone. The portable one: carry it and it keeps playing
  • SpeakerGold, a note block, iron and redstone. Point it at a jukebox or boombox to repeat that sound further away, and a redstone signal mutes it
  • Disc PedestalGold and planks. Displays a disc on a stand instead of hiding it in a chest
  • Disc Dyeing TableAny dye over planks. Colours discs and albums so you can tell them apart at a glance

Making a disc

Place the Music Disc Maker and open it. Paste a link into the box, drop a Blank Disc into the slot, and you get a Custom Music Disc that remembers the track's title, artist and length. YouTube links and direct links to an audio file both work. SoundCloud usually does not — it tends to hand out a thirty-second preview rather than the track, and the mod will tell you so.

If a link fails, the message says why rather than making you guess: private or deleted, age-restricted, blocked in your region, or the site asking the server to prove it is not a bot. Any of those means try a different link, not that anything is broken.

Albums

An album is one item holding several discs. Craft it, open it, and put discs in; dye it at the Disc Dyeing Table so the blue one is obvious at a glance. Put the album into a Golden Jukebox and the next, previous and shuffle buttons work across the whole thing, so you get a playlist rather than one song on repeat.

Two limits are worth knowing before you fill one. An album has a size budget rather than a simple slot count, so a handful of discs that each hold many tracks can fill it before the slots run out — “too much disc data to store” means exactly that, and swapping one crowded disc for a shorter one fixes it. And if an album ever refuses to open because it is too large, sneak and use it while aiming at the air: the discs drop at your feet undamaged and you can rebuild it.

Playing it where people can hear

A Golden Jukebox has a range setting and a directional toggle: leave it positional and the sound comes from the box, or turn it off and it fills the whole radius evenly, which is the one you want for a shop or a bar. A Speaker linked to that jukebox repeats it further out, and wiring redstone to the speaker mutes it — a lever by the door is enough to turn the music off without going back to the box.

The audio is streamed, not stored. A disc holds the link, not the song, so it plays by fetching the track at the moment you press play. A video that gets taken down stops working, and so does a disc made from a link that was never public. Nothing is downloaded into your world save.

Everyone needs the same pack version. This mod refuses to play along with a server running a different build of it, so if your discs do nothing while everybody else's work, run the updater before looking for any other explanation.
