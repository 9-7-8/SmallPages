In-game guides

Nothing on this site goes deep, and it doesn't need to: most of the big mods here ship their own manual inside the game. Four separate guidebook systems are installed, so “where do I even start with this mod” usually has an answer three keypresses away.

The books

  • PatchouliThe common one. A mod's book is a craftable item — search the mod's name in the recipe viewer — and it fills itself in as you progress
  • ModonomiconThe same idea with a progress map. Occultism's Dictionary of Spirits and Theurgy's book both use it
  • GuideMEApplied Energistics 2's manual. Its blocks and items link into the relevant page rather than dumping you at the front
  • Oracle IndexA wiki browser inside the game, pulling pages from moddedmc.wiki. Covers mods that never wrote a book of their own

If a mod has a book, read the first two pages of it before searching the internet. Pack versions drift from whatever a three-year-old video shows, and the book in your hand is generated from the recipes this server is actually running.

Tooltips earn their keep

A lot of what you'd otherwise have to look up is already attached to the item.

  • Enchantment DescriptionsWhat an enchantment actually does, written into its tooltip instead of implied by a name
  • Better Advanced TooltipsItem ids, durability, repair cost and burn time, without F3
  • Just Enough ProfessionsWhich villager profession sells a thing, shown next to the trade
  • PolymorphWhen two mods claim the same crafting grid, a chooser appears instead of one silently winning
  • Tip of the LoomLoom patterns, labelled

The quest book is the pack's own guide. FTB Quests is less a list of chores than a reading order — it points at which mod to pick up next and hands you the starting items for it. Nothing in this pack is gated behind a quest, so treat it as a suggestion.
