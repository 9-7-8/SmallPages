Horses

The horses here are not vanilla horses. Procedural Horse Coats gives every one of them a real genome — coat, build, temperament and stats, all of it inherited by its foals. It's written by the person running this server and it updates most days, so the mod's own wiki is the source of truth, not this page. This tab is just enough to get you started.

The controls

  • E, from the saddleThat horse's own menu — genes, family, gear. Press it again to close
  • HThe horse browser: every horse you own
  • RAttach or detach a cart from the horse you're riding

The saddle menu follows whatever your Inventory key is, so if you've moved it off E, use the key you moved it to. Donkeys and mules still open their ordinary inventory. If one of these does nothing at all it's more likely unbound than broken — KeybindsPurger strips conflicting defaults, so check Options → Controls first.

Keeping one alive

Two things are worth knowing before you own a horse, because neither is vanilla:

  1. A hurt horse needs water nearby and a full stomach to heal.

    Water within three blocks, and not starving. Vanilla's free trickle of regeneration is switched off for horses, so a hungry horse stays hurt until you feed it. Leave water and something to graze in the pen and it sorts itself out.

  2. They graze, so fence your farm.

    A horse eats crops whole and turns grass to dirt the way a sheep does. It won't reach through a fence, but an open gate is an open invitation.

A horse is hard to lose now. Nothing kills one in a single blow — a killing hit leaves it on half a heart with a few seconds of immunity instead. Below a fifth of its health it stops answering the reins and bolts, carrying you out rather than throwing you. Let it run.

Your own horses also talk to you in chat: what hurt one and what to do about it, who lost one and to what, and why a mare wasn't covered.
