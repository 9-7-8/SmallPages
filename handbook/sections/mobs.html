Mobs & dangers

Five rules on this server change how dangerous the world is, and none of them are vanilla behaviour you can look up elsewhere. They are all here because somebody asked.

Hostile mobs will not start a fight with you while the sun is up. From dawn to dusk — the clock, not the weather, so a thunderstorm at noon is still safe — nothing picks a fight it wasn't already in.

Neutral, not passive. Hit something and it fights back properly. You simply cannot be jumped by something you never touched. The exceptions are the ones you brought on yourself: the Wither and the Ender Dragon always fight, raiders during a raid always fight, and an iron golem will still come for you if you hit a villager. The Nether and the End have no day, so nothing changes there.

The other four

  • CreepersStill explode, still hurt, still drop their loot — but they do not take your blocks with them. Only creepers; endermen, villagers and withers behave as normal
  • PhantomsOff. You will never be attacked for not sleeping
  • Powder snowYou do not sink into it and it does not freeze you. Horses too
  • Suffocating in a wallIf a block is about to kill you — or kill a horse — you get pulled out and put somewhere safe. It waits until you are nearly dead on purpose, so ordinary crawlspaces don't teleport you about

The dinosaurs

Eight prehistoric types from Fossil's Arisen and Primal Frontier — megaraptor, carnotaurus, ornithomimus and friends — follow two extra rules, both of which cost ten horses to learn:

  • They only spawn naturally at night. Hatching one from an egg or spawning one deliberately works at any hour.
  • They will only ever hunt hostile mobs — never you, never a horse, never another passive animal. A megaraptor loose in your paddock is a guard, not a problem.

What else lives out there

  • Alex's MobsDozens of new animals with real behaviours, spread across every biome. Many drop something worth having
  • Ice and FireDragons, hippogryphs, cyclopes and dragon caves. The dragons are a serious fight and their hoards are worth it
  • Dragon Mounts ReforgedHatch a dragon egg, raise it, ride it. The reward for the vanilla dragon finally being useful
  • Cave DwellerSomething stalks you underground, and it is genuinely unpleasant. It cannot break blocks or doors here — that was a condition of installing it — so a closed door is a real answer
  • Spider OverhaulSpiders behave differently and come in variants. Weaker Spider Webs means cobwebs no longer glue you in place
  • Easy Villagers & Easy PiglinsPick a villager up, carry it home, trade through a block. Removes the worst chore in the game
  • Wither Skeleton TweaksSkulls drop at a sane rate, so a beacon is a project rather than a lottery

Mobs more than 128 blocks from everyone get swept on the hour, with two warnings first. That is further than you can see, but it does mean a chamber that holds mobs alive will empty itself when you walk away. A farm that kills them into a chest is fine. Full detail on Quality of life.
