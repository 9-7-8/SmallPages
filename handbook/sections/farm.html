Farming & food

Food here is not only hunger. Eating a variety of things is how you get more than ten hearts, and about a dozen mods stack new crops, trees, bees and machines on top of vanilla's farm.

Your maximum health comes from the number of different foods you have eaten. Everyone starts at ten hearts. Every time your count of distinct foods reaches 5, 10, 15, 20 and 25, you gain two more hearts — twenty hearts once you've eaten twenty-five different things. It carries through death, so the progress is never lost, and it is the single cheapest way to get tougher early. Eat the new crop rather than selling all of it.

This is Spice of Life: Carrot Edition. Your food book tracks what you've had.

Where the new crops come from

  • CroptopiaThe big one — dozens of crops and hundreds of recipes made from them. Most of your food variety will come from here
  • Productive FarmingMore crops and flowers, and the feedstock for the Productive line of mods
  • Primal FrontierA harsher, earlier-game set of crops and tools. Also where the megaraptor comes from
  • Pastry GardenStrawberry cows and the sweet end of farming
  • Haunted HarvestCorn, pumpkin carving, and villagers who come trick-or-treating
  • FieldsLarge cultivated fields that generate in plains — free farmland if you find one
  • PantryworkThe glue. A shared set of food tags, so one mod's tomato works in another mod's recipe and you never have to hunt the “right” one

Making a farm worth automating

  • Mystical AgricultureGrow resources as crops — iron, diamond, almost anything — with Agradditions and the Growth Accelerator tiers on top. The usual answer to “I need ten thousand of these”
  • AgriTech: EvolvedAgricultural automation proper: machines and modules that plant, harvest and process, with compatibility for most of the crops above
  • Farming for BlockheadsThe market, where you can simply buy seeds and animals, plus fertilised farmland and a chicken nest
  • Cooking for BlockheadsA kitchen that works as one unit — connect the appliances and cook from any ingredient within reach, rather than carrying stacks to a crafting grid
  • Instant ReplantHarvesting a crop replants it. Removes most of the tedium from a hand-worked farm
  • Crops Love RainRain speeds growth up, so a wet week is a good week to plant

Bees, trees, fish

Productive Bees is the bee progression — dozens of species, each producing something different, with ModularBees adding a multiblock hive once one box stops being enough. Productive Trees does the same for wood and fruit. For fishing, Aquaculture 2 rebuilds the whole system with new rods, bait and a great many fish; Lava Fishing is exactly what it says; and BBL Strainers sit in water collecting drops in the background while you do something else.

Fence your farm before you own a horse. Horses here graze — a crop is eaten whole and grass turns to dirt. They won't reach through a fence, but an open gate is an invitation. See Horses.
