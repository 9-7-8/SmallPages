Dimensions & dungeons

The overworld is three terrain mods deep, there are two whole dimensions beyond the Nether and the End, and almost every structure you walk into was built by a mod rather than by Mojang. Worth going out.

Two new dimensions

  • The Aether IIFloating islands above the clouds. The way in is a glowstone portal frame filled with water, which is the one thing worth knowing before you read anything else. A full progression of its own waits on the other side
  • The UndergardenA deep, bioluminescent cave dimension with its own ores, mobs and bosses. It has its own portal — look up the frame block in the recipe viewer

The overworld you already have

Terralith, Tectonic and Regions Unexplored all generate at once, and Nullscape replaces the End — the Getting around page has the links. The practical consequence is that biomes are far stranger and much larger than vanilla, so Nature's Compass (find a biome) and Explorer's Compass (find a structure) are two of the most useful items in the pack. Craft both early.

Do not use /locate, and nobody needs to. One /locate structure call on this pack took 39 seconds of server time and disconnected the player who ran it — with 249 structure types installed it is simply too expensive. Instead, ask in chat: type claude where is the nearest ancient city and you'll get an answer, computed from the map files at no cost to the server. It only knows terrain somebody has already explored, so “nothing found” means “not nearby yet”.

Structures worth looking for

  • Dungeon CrawlHuge multi-layer dungeons under the surface. The spiritual successor to Roguelike Dungeons and the best loot-per-hour in the pack
  • YUNG's Better DungeonsVanilla dungeons, completely redesigned and actually worth clearing
  • Much More DungeonsA broad spread of smaller themed dungeons, balanced for exploring rather than grinding
  • Underground VillagesVillages below the surface, with villagers who never asked for sunlight
  • Overhauled Villages & Towns and TowersVillages and outposts rebuilt per biome, so a village is a destination rather than four huts
  • LootrThe quiet hero of a shared server: every player opens their own copy of a structure chest. Nobody has to rush ahead and nobody gets an empty dungeon

Mark things as you find them. JourneyMap waypoints cost a keypress and save an hour, and they can be shared with your team — see Getting around. The live map at ixoraatm1.lodeway.app shows the explored world in a browser.
