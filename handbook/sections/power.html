Power & automation

There is no “which energy system should I pick” question here. Every power mod in the pack speaks the same Forge Energy, so a generator from one mod runs a machine from another down a cable from a third. Build whatever looks good and it will connect.

Making power

  • PowahThe easy start. Furnators and thermal generators early, reactors and solar panels later, plus the best energy cells in the pack
  • Generator GaloreOne generator for nearly every fuel you might already have spare
  • Energized PowerA broad machine set with a clean tier ladder — a good spine if you want one mod to do most things
  • OritechNewer, and visually the nicest of them. Worth a look before you commit to a factory layout
  • Ender IOConduits that carry power, items, fluids and redstone in one block, and the machines to go with them
  • Productive MetalworksMelting and casting metal. Pairs with Silent Gear, which wants cast parts — see Gear & combat

Moving things about

  • PipezStart here. One pipe per type, upgrades for speed and filtering, and almost no configuration to get wrong
  • Classic PipesThe old-school routing feel, if you grew up on BuildCraft
  • LaserIOOne node per machine and a card for each job, with laser connectors instead of a pipe run. Compact and fast
  • Modular RoutersA single block you fit modules into — breaker, placer, vacuum, sender. Often one router replaces a whole contraption
  • Logistics NetworksHigh-level item logistics built to be cheap on the server

Telling it what to do

Super Factory Manager is the one to learn if you like writing rules rather than laying pipe: label your inventories, write a short program, and one manager runs the whole factory. Integrated Dynamics (with Tunnels, Crafting and Terminals) is the heavier, more powerful version of the same idea. More Red adds logic gate plates you rotate by poking them with a stick, for the times the answer is redstone rather than software. Energy Meter clamps onto a cable and tells you what is actually flowing, which settles most “why is this slow” arguments in one look.

Spending the power

Iron Jetpacks are the usual first luxury — a tiered, FE-powered flight upgrade path. Charging Gadgets keeps your tools and jetpack topped up without a machine for each. Modular Force Field System builds actual force fields, which is a defence and a very good wall. Quarries, storage networks and sorting all have their own pages: see Quarries, Mining and Sorting chests.

A machine that does nothing is almost always power or a face. Look at the block — Jade's tooltip shows its energy buffer and status — and check which side you piped into. Second most common cause is a full output with nowhere to go.

Keep it inside your claim. Claim protection covers machines, containers and storage networks, including AE2 systems. An unclaimed factory is not protected by anything. See Claiming land.
