Magic

Eight separate magic mods are installed and none of them need each other. That is good news: pick one, read its book, and get somewhere. Trying to open all eight at once is the reliable way to bounce off every one of them.

Pick one of these

  • OccultismSummoning. Ritual circles, demons who do work for you, familiars that follow you about, and dimensional storage that beats a wall of chests. Its Dictionary of Spirits is one of the best written books in the pack — the recommended first magic mod
  • TheurgyClassical alchemy. Mercury, salts, transmuting one material into another and replicating what you already have. Heavily automatable, which makes it the most tech-like of them
  • Forbidden & ArcanusArtefacts and a darker flavour, built around the Clibano furnace and a long list of things to craft. Patchouli book
  • Neo VitaeBlood magic. An altar, a blood orb, sigils and rituals, paid for in health — yours at first, something else's later
  • EvilCraftThe other blood mod, and a weirder one. Blood, dark power and a lot of cursed items
  • Roots ClassicDruidic. Spells assembled out of herbs and rituals performed with plants. Low-tech and quick to start
  • Mahou TsukaiFlashy. Projected weapons and enormous magic circles, loosely inspired by Fate/stay night. Not a progression mod so much as a box of spectacular tools
  • WizardsThe spellcaster. Arcane, fire and frost spells cast from a bar, learned through a skill tree, with Runes as ammunition. See Skills & levelling — the trees are where the spells come from

Odds and ends worth knowing

Reliquary is magic-flavoured equipment rather than a progression: pedestals, charms and a handful of genuinely strong items that live in your Curios slots. Pylons are single blocks with an area effect — harvesting, expulsion, interdiction — useful around a base whether or not you ever touch the rest of this page. Apotheosis straddles magic and gear; its enchanting side is on the Gear & combat page.

Spirits, rituals and altars are blocks like any other, and claim protection treats them that way. Build the ritual circle inside your claim. A summoning that needs a chalk circle on the ground outside it is a summoning somebody else can walk over.
