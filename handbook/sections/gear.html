Gear & combat

Diamond to netherite is one ladder here and not the most interesting one. Nothing is locked behind a character level — see Skills & levelling — so what you can hold is decided by what you can make.

Building your own weapon

Silent Gear is the deep one: every tool is assembled from parts, and the material of each part decides the stats, so a sword is a recipe rather than a tier. Silent's Gems feeds it new materials. Silent Gear Metalworks changes one rule you should know up front — any part made of a meltable material has to be cast in a foundry rather than crafted in a grid, which means a little Productive Metalworks setup before you can make good gear. That is deliberate, not a bug.

Or wear something made for the job

  • Epic KnightsPlate armour, kite shields, pikes, halberds and arming swords. The best-looking armour in the pack and genuinely strong, with Knight Helmets adding more heads
  • ApotheosisAffixed loot. Dropped and chest weapons come with rolled bonuses and rarities, and the enchanting table becomes a real system with eterna, quanta and arcana to manage
  • Nemo's EnchantmentsA long list of extra enchantments, described in their own tooltips
  • Iron's Arms 'n ArtificeHistorical-fantasy firearms, built as an artificer rather than looted
  • Critical StrikeMelee and ranged attacks can crit on chance, so attack speed and luck both start to matter

The slots you did not know you had

Curios adds the equipment slots nearly every other mod here hangs things off — rings, belts, charms, back slots. Open your inventory and look for the extra tab.

  • Iron's Gems 'n JewelryJewelcrafting: cut gems, set them into rings and necklaces, and choose what they do
  • ArtifactsUncraftable curios found while exploring. Several are better than anything you can make at the same point
  • Nameless TrinketsPowerful, strange trinkets from exploration. Read what they do before you rely on one
  • Baubley Heart CanistersExtra hearts in a curio slot, stacking with the food-variety hearts on the Farming & food page
  • TransmogMake a good item look like a different one without changing its stats. Keep the plate armour look, keep the netherite numbers

Fights worth picking

Gateways to Eternity is the one to aim for once you are kitted out: place a gate, survive the waves it spawns, and take the rewards. Apothic Spawners makes captured spawners modifiable, which is how most people end up farming a specific mob. And Training Dummy is quietly one of the most useful blocks in the pack — hit it to see your real damage per second, so you can compare two builds instead of guessing.

Dying is not a disaster. Your things go into a grave block where you fell, not onto the floor, and the hourly item sweep never touches graves. Walk back and collect it — see Quality of life.

Mobs will not start a fight with you in daylight. That changes how you should gear up; the detail is on Mobs & dangers.
