PDF Builder

Put documents in, take one document out. Every page of everything you drop in turns up as a thumbnail you can drag into order, delete, rotate or duplicate, and what you are left with saves as a single PDF. PDFs keep their pages exactly as they were — the real page is copied over, fonts and all, not a picture of it. Word, OpenDocument, Markdown and plain text are set as fresh pages here, because they have no pages of their own until something lays them out.

A copied page is the page, not a picture of it. When a PDF goes in, its pages are carried across whole — the text stays text, the fonts come with it, and anything searchable stays searchable. That is the difference between this and rasterising a document: nothing is re-encoded, so nothing is lost and the file does not balloon. Rotation is recorded as the page's own rotation rather than by redrawing it, which is why it costs nothing and can be undone by turning it back.

Word and OpenDocument have no pages until something makes them. A .docx is a zip of XML describing a flow of paragraphs; where the page breaks fall is decided by whatever opens it. So those are laid out here — unzipped in the page, the paragraphs read out, headings kept as headings, and the text set onto fresh pages at the size you choose. It is a faithful reading of the words and an honest admission about everything else: columns, tables, floated images and precise spacing are not reproduced. If a document's layout matters, export it to PDF from the program that made it and drop that in instead.

A long document is the ordinary case. Three hundred pages behave the same as three: a thumbnail is drawn only when it scrolls into view, and the drawings are forgotten again once a few hundred of them have been made, so what the tab holds is the documents themselves rather than a picture of every page in them. Nothing here caps how many pages you can drop in or how many can come out.

Everything happens in this tab. The zip reading is the browser's own DecompressionStream; the PDF work is pdf-lib for building and pdf.js for the thumbnails, both vendored in this repository rather than fetched from anywhere. No file is uploaded and nothing leaves the page.
