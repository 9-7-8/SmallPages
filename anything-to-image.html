anything → image

Paste, drop, or pick anything at all — a screenshot, a cell range out of a spreadsheet, a shape off a slide, an SVG, a paragraph of text, a link — and it comes back as an image you can copy or save. Drop a whole .xlsx and every tab comes back as its own page, trimmed to the cells that have something in them.
