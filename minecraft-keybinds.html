Ixora Keybinds

Every keybinding in the pack — 481 of them — and where to find it. Most are not on a key at all: they live on a wheel, and you reach them by holding ` (the key left of 1) and picking. 58 have a key of their own, and 86 are sharing on purpose — F3 combinations, mouse buttons, and the hold-this-while-you-click modifiers.

These wheels have a key of their own, once you bind them in Options › Controls: Applied Energistics, Crafting tweaks, Horses, Map, Quests, Recipes, Sophisticated Backpacks, Voice chat. Everything else is under Everything A–H, I–Q or R–W on the main wheel, grouped by the mod it belongs to.
