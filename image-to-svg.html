What to draw

Palette

Locked colours stay exactly where you put them and the rest of the palette is fitted around them. Editing a swatch re-maps the image without re-reading it, so you can nudge a shade without losing the others.

Ink

Centre lines thin the dark areas down to a single-pixel skeleton and follow it, so a drawn stroke becomes one path rather than a loop around its edge. It suits pen work, handwriting and logos; photographs will come out as noise.

Detail

“Tidy up edges” replaces each pixel with the most common colour around it. It removes the blended band along anti-aliased edges, which would otherwise be traced as a thin halo around every shape. Turn it down if it is eating detail you want.

Trace resolution is how big the image is when it gets read, not the size of the SVG — the output always matches the original dimensions. Higher is more faithful and much slower.
