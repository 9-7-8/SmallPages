Sleep Calculator

Pick a time to wake up, or a time to go to bed, and get the times at the other end that land between sleep cycles rather than in the middle of one — which is the difference between waking up and being woken up.

minutes — 90 is the usual average

It is a rule of thumb, not a measurement. Ninety minutes is an average of a thing that varies: a real cycle runs anywhere from about 70 to 120 minutes, it changes through the night — the early ones are shorter — and it changes with age and with how short you were the night before. Nothing here knows which one you are having. Treat the times as somewhere to aim rather than an alarm to trust, and move the cycle length above if you have ever measured your own.

Fifteen minutes to fall asleep is the figure most sleep advice uses, and it is built into every time below rather than left for you to subtract. If you know you take longer, say so and everything shifts with it.

Five or six cycles — seven and a half to nine hours — is the band most adults are aiming at, so those two are the ones lit. Four is a short night and three is a nap you will feel; they are listed because some nights are not most nights.
