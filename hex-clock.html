Hex Clock

00:00:00

The time is the colour. The six digits on the clock are the six digits of the hex, in that order and nothing else: at 14:23:45 the page behind them is #142345. Twenty-four hour, zero-padded, local time.

It can never be bright. No hour passes 23 and no minute or second passes 59, so red stops at 0x23 and green and blue at 0x59. Midnight is pure black, 23:59:59 is the lightest the day gets, and the page only ever reaches 86,400 of the 16,777,216 colours — one for each second of the day.
